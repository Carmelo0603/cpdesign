# Surface: public portfolio (home, archive, project detail, about) + admin

Scope: whole site. Public routes in Experience mode (the work leads); admin in Operate mode inside the same world, restrained.
Audience: recruiters and tech leads deciding in under a minute. Actions: open a project, download the CV, send a message.
Proof: real projects from Supabase, education timeline, skills. No testimonials or metrics exist; none are invented.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio as an independent design magazine. A full-colour editorial world where Carmelo's path from graphic design to code is told through authored illustrations rather than a portrait. Chosen by the user on 2026-11-12 (direction A, "Editoriale a tutto colore", after a Dribbble/Behance research pass), with illustrations in place of a profile photo.

OWN-WORLD: Warm newsprint paper (#F2EDE3) and ink (#16150F); full fields of Electric Blue (#2340FF), Press Yellow (#FFD23F) and Signal Orange (#FF5B35). Archivo variable (condensed 72% uppercase for mastheads, 700 for headlines) paired with Instrument Serif italic as the personal voice. Pills for actions and tags; square coloured plates behind every project image. Flat, no shadows.

STORY: The cover (blue) gives the name, the statement and a curve becoming code. Then the latest project as a spread, two more as an asymmetric pair, the journey in three illustrated chapters, the skills index on yellow, and the contact band on blue.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Paper header (wordmark, nav, IT/EN, CV pill). Blue cover: role and availability pill, the CARMELO / PROVENZANI masthead, the serif statement, the lead, and the light "Guarda i progetti" and outline "Scarica il CV" buttons in the left 7 columns. The CurveToCode illustration fills the right 5 columns and draws on load.

FORM: Replaces the Unigrid transit-map world (seed:da6d0039082d3ed2) in full.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
